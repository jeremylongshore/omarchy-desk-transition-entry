# Test Personas

- Docking developer: moves between a laptop panel and multiple active desk
  displays and needs repeatable layout without losing an output.
- Safety-conscious operator: will not accept hard-coded connector assumptions,
  shell injection, hidden network access, or a command that disables a screen.
- Keyboard and assistive-technology user: needs named scene controls, close and
  panel navigation, and an announced outcome after each action.
- Marketplace maintainer: needs exact copy limits, unique authored art, current
  static validation, real-shell proof, and a screenshot bound to source.
- Hostile local-state tester: supplies malformed, oversized, duplicate, slow,
  or numerically extreme Hyprland monitor data.
